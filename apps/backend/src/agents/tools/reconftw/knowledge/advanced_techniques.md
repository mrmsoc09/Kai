ReconFTW writes multiple files and summary lines. Parse summary/subdomain artifacts for machine-routing while preserving raw artifacts.

## Supplemental Use
Treat ReconFTW discoveries as candidate deltas; validate against combined known asset lists before promotion.

## Replacement Warning
