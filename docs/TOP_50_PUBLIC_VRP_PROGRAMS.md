## LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR K1

Before scanning, ensure:

- ✅ Program scope includes domains you're targeting
- ✅ You have explicit permission (check program rules)
- ✅ You respect rate limiting and don't cause DoS
- ✅ You don't test for accounts takeover (report instead)
- ✅ You disclose responsibly (follow responsible disclosure policy)
- ✅ You don't search for or access non-public data
- ✅ You document your methodology
- ✅ You test on staging/test environments when possible
