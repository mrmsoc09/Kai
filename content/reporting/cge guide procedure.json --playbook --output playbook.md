
## Content Types

### Intelligence Reports
- Support for classification levels (UNCLASSIFIED through TOP SECRET)
- Source reliability ratings
- Confidence assessments
- Annex handling

### Contracting Documents
- RFP/RFQ response generation
- Past performance documentation
- Technical approach formatting
- Compliance matrix generation

### Threat Intelligence
- IOC (Indicator of Compromise) management
- STIX 2.1 bundle generation
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Actor and TTP documentation

### E-Books
- Multi-chapter support
- Table of contents generation
- Front matter and back matter
- Word count tracking

### Guides & Playbooks
- Step-by-step procedures
- Operational playbooks with escalation paths
- Tutorials with exercises
- Checklist generation
