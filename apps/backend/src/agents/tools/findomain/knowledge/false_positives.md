# Findomain False Positives

## Historical CT Residue
Expired or decommissioned domains may remain visible due to certificate history.

## Wildcard Interference
Wildcard DNS can inflate perceived surface area. Cross-check random labels and compare answer consistency.

## Out-of-Scope Collateral
When expanding enumeration scope, related properties can appear. Enforce scope policy before active scanning.

## Noise Handling
Demote generic infrastructure labels when they provide low exploit value unless corroborated by additional evidence.
